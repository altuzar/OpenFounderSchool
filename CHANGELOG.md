# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-28

English edition: translated the complete public curriculum, generated lessons,
annual guides, handbooks, research notes, mentor roles, templates, prompts,
platform adapters, examples, CLI messages, and downloadable kits. English is
the default teaching language. Retained the curriculum structure, source
provenance, financial definitions, privacy rules, and validation limits.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-28

First independent edition, originally published in Spanish: 160-week
after-school path, 40 first-year lessons, original readings, handbooks, eight
AI coaching roles, local human mentoring, templates, and offline tooling.
Manual adapters for Grok Bot, Claude Project, ChatGPT Work, Pi, and Hermes.

Research covered 29 public sitemap URLs with documented limits and 21 channel
publications through complete automatic transcripts and visual sampling.
Five reproducible kits; code and link checks. No student pilot or behavioral
validation in provider accounts.
