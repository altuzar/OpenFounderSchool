# OpenFounderSchool

A free, independent, Spanish-first after-school entrepreneurship curriculum that families can run in their own AI assistant. Ask the coach to teach in English if preferred.

**“Graduate with US$1M in profit or get your tuition back. Tuition is US$0.”** This is a humorous tuition promise, not an earnings guarantee: the maximum refund is US$0. Third-party costs are excluded. The target means cumulative net profit in USD, not revenue, valuation or personal wealth. See [the complete explanation](docs/GUARANTEE.md).

Start with [START-HERE](START-HERE.md), select one of the five [platform adapters](adapters/README.md), load [COACH.md](prompts/COACH.md), and ask: “Start year 1, week 1 in English. Use simulation, no external actions, no spending, and ask onboarding questions one at a time.”

Four years, 40 weeks each, six hours a week. Year 1 has detailed lessons; years 2–4 have weekly projects and assessment. Eight AI coaching roles are fictional functional roles, not impersonations of real mentors. Human supervision remains necessary.

Provider subscriptions and optional business costs are separate. [Research and coverage](research/README.md) describe what was reviewed and what was not.
