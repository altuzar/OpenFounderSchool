# Working agreement for families and agents

These are program rules, not a legal opinion about any country. Account, payment, child labor, data, contract, and tax requirements vary. The adult verifies applicable requirements before operating.

| Activity | Initial mode | Before actual activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interviewing | Roleplay with AI or family | Adult-reviewed contacts and script |
| Presenting an offer | Private draft | Authorized scope, remedy, price, and recipient |
| Charging money | Labeled fictional receipt | Account and terms appropriately managed by an adult |
| Building software | Local files and synthetic data | Privacy, access, tests, and cost review before deployment |
| Publishing content | Private portfolio | Review of data, rights, and promises before publication |
| Automating | One manual execution | Explicit request, budget, and stop mechanism |
| Human mentoring | Session with adult present | mentors/HUMANS.md protocol |

## Practical rules

- Do not collect sensitive student or customer data for practice. Use aliases and summarized amounts.
- Never put keys in Markdown, CSV, repositories, or conversations. An adult manages secrets using appropriate tools.
- Do not train the student's writing style by reading the family's entire mailbox. Three fictional or reviewed personal samples are enough.
- Do not use real users' screenshots without permission. Add no invented testimonials or other people's case figures to your own pitch.
- Do not arrange visits, deliveries, calls, or meetings with strangers without adult coordination. Digital and simulated alternatives exist.
- Do not test security on third-party systems. A vulnerable site does not grant permission to scan it.
- Do not name a bot after a celebrity as if it were that person or promise access to them.
- Do not use guilt, humiliation, wealth rankings, or monitoring outside sessions.

## If data is exposed

Stop publication or the integration, notify the adult, and remove access where possible. If a key was exposed, revoke it with its provider; deleting the text is insufficient. Retain only minimal incident evidence privately. Follow appropriate service or institutional procedures. Do not republish personal information in a public issue when seeking help.

The agent must not explore other family files to “know the student better.” Limit its accessible folder. System permissions and accounts are the real barrier; these instructions do not guarantee a model will always comply.
