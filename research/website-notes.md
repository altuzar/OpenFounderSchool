OpenFounderSchool provides its own short readings and activities. Buying or reading a book selection is not presumed to produce a financial outcome.

## Limits that shape the design

The reference school combines staff, academic technology, and an in-person environment. A prompt file does not reproduce that supervision, network, or institution. The open project therefore identifies what a family supplies, what a human mentor may contribute, and what is only an AI role. Results mentioned by the school do not become results of this edition.
