# Problem interview

Mode: SIMULATION / REAL. Contact: alias; never public identifying details.
Adult permission for this conversation, date, and channel:

1. Tell me about the last time this problem happened.
2. What did you do to solve it and how long did it take?
3. What was hardest? What would happen if you did nothing?
4. Have you paid to solve it? Who decides and with what budget?
5. What relevant question did I miss?

Keep brief direct observations privately, interpretations separately, possible
biases, contrary evidence, and the next question. Do not sell during an
interview without agreement. Collect no sensitive data or recordings by default.
